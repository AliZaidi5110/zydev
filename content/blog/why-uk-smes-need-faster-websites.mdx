If your website takes more than a couple of seconds to become useful, you’re paying for it — in rankings, ad quality scores, and lost enquiries.

## Speed is a trust signal

UK customers compare you to the best experience they’ve had today, not the average site in your industry. A slow first load feels like an unreliable business.

## What actually moves LCP

- Compress and correctly size hero images
- Avoid heavy client-side rendering for marketing pages
- Prefetch fonts with `next/font` to prevent layout shift
- Keep third-party scripts on a diet
