Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) remains one of the highest-ROI channels for local UK businesses.

## Complete the basics

1. Choose the most accurate primary category
2. Add secondary categories only when relevant
3. Write a keyword-aware but natural business description
4. Upload real photos of your premises, team, and work

## Build trust signals

Encourage recent reviews, reply to every one, and keep opening hours accurate — especially around bank holidays.

## Connect the rest of your digital footprint

Your website, citations, and social profiles should share consistent name, address, and phone details. Inconsistency quietly erodes local rankings.
